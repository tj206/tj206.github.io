Now

I’m a communications and outreach intern at the UN Department of Global Communications, in the Human Rights and Outreach Programmes Section, where my work focuses on the Outreach Programme on the Transatlantic Slave Trade and Slavery. I write for the programme’s monthly newsletter, rewrite public web content, draft speeches and talking points for senior staff, and help build the events that bring the programme’s mandate into a room. The work centres on research, editing, and judgment about what general readers are able to digest.

United Nations DGC·September 2026–present·5,100+ subscribersOngoing

In September I started contributing to the programme’s newsletter. I researched and wrote much of the September issue, built around the International Day for People of African Descent and the twenty-fifth anniversary of the Durban Declaration, and I’m now helping produce the October issue. Background page, Remember Slavery

United Nations DGC·2026In draft

Visitors to the site often arrive without the vocabulary or historical context the rest of it assumes. I’m writing a background page covering the duration of the trade, its scale and distances, the countries and destinations involved, and the difference between abolishing the trade and abolishing slavery. Everything is drawn from UN sources I researched, with inline citations and Chicago-style references.

Transatlantic slave trade timeline

Self-initiated·2026In development

I proposed it to my supervisor, then researched and built it on my own: a chronology of the trade and its abolition, written entry by entry, intended to sit alongside the existing Remember Slavery pages. Publication is under consideration.

On & Off the Field with Noah Miles

Black Student Resource Commons, University of San Diego·November 2022

A seven-question interview with a football player and behavioral neuroscience major. Topics include being the only athlete in his classes, the brotherhood of a team, the assumptions people bring to student-athletes, coaches who do and don’t understand him, and fitting labs around practice. I wrote the questions, conducted the interview, and edited his answers into a carousel for the Commons’ Instagram.
